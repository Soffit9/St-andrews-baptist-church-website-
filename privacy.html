PRIVACY

Privacy Notice

What we collect, who sees it, and how to have it removed.

This website is run by St. Andrews Baptist Church, 115 King Street, St. Andrews, New Brunswick. It is hosted on a computer owned by the church, kept at the church building. We are not a business and we do not sell anything or advertise on this site.

Prayer requests

If you send a prayer request, we keep what you type in the form: your request, and — only if you choose to give them — your name and email address. You may leave both blank and send it anonymously.

  • Who reads it: Pastor Ladd Dunfield and members of the church admin team.
  • If you ticked "pray this aloud": it may be read out in the prayer meeting, where other members of the congregation will hear it. If you did not tick it, it stays with the pastor and admin team.
  • We do not sell it, share it outside the church, publish it on this website, or use it to send you anything.
  • Please be careful about sharing someone else's private information — a medical condition, for example — without asking them first.

Website visits

We count how many people visit the site each day. This is done without storing anything that identifies you: your address and browser are combined with today's date and scrambled into a short code that cannot be reversed. Because the date is part of it, the same visitor produces a completely different code tomorrow — so we can count visitors without being able to follow anyone over time.

We do not use Google Analytics or any advertising or tracking service.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies for ordinary visitors. A single cookie is used only when a church admin logs in to the private admin area, and only to keep them logged in.

Some pages include content from other companies — a Google map on the Contact page, and YouTube or Facebook video players on the Sermons page. Those companies may set their own cookies when their content loads, under their own privacy policies rather than ours. Video players are loaded in privacy-enhanced mode where the provider offers it.

Church admin accounts

People who help run the site have an account holding their name, email address and permission level. Passwords are stored scrambled and cannot be read by anyone, including us.

Having your information removed

You can ask us to delete anything you have sent — including a prayer request — at any time, and you do not need to give a reason. Contact the church by phone or email and we will remove it.

Prayer requests are kept only as long as they are useful to the prayer team, and are deleted after that.

Questions

If you have a question or concern about anything on this page, please get in touch — by phone at (506) 529-3022 or through the contact page. We would rather hear from you than have you wonder.

This notice describes how the website actually works, in plain language. It was written by the volunteer who built the site, not by a lawyer. If the church wants formal legal assurance, it should be reviewed by someone qualified.
