How Do I…?

Everything you can do on this site, in plain language. Nothing here can break anything — every change is recorded in the Change Log and can be undone.

Nothing matches that. Try a shorter word, or ask Dante.

Post this week's sermon

Dashboard → Sermon Archive. The blue box at the top is the quick way.

  1. Pick the date of the service.
  2. Type the title, the speaker, and the Bible passage.
  3. Paste the video link — a YouTube link or a Facebook video link both work. Copy it straight from the address bar.
  4. Leave "Show this as the latest sermon" ticked.
  5. Click Post Sermon.

That one step files it in the archive and puts it on the homepage as the newest sermon. You don't need to go anywhere else.

Untick the box when you're catching up on older sermons you missed — they'll go into the archive without replacing the current one on the homepage.

Add or change an event

Dashboard → Events. You'll see a calendar.

  1. Click the day the event happens.
  2. Fill in the title, the time, and a short description.
  3. Click Apply Changes.

More than one thing happening that day? Click + Add Another Event This Day before saving.

Events show on the Events page and the homepage automatically. Once the date passes they drop off on their own — you don't have to tidy up after them.

The Church Calendar tile is a different thing: that's for who's doing what on a Sunday (prayer, sound, greeting), and it's not shown to the public.

Update someone's contact info, or add a new person

Dashboard → Who's Who.

  1. Find the person. The search box at the top matches names, roles, and groups.
  2. Change their phone or email in the boxes.
  3. Click Apply Changes at the bottom.

For somebody new, click + Add Person, fill in their name, role, group, phone and email, then Apply Changes.

Whatever group you put someone in is what the filter buttons on the public Who's Who page use — and the "Need to reach someone?" buttons on the homepage jump straight to those groups.

Only put contact details on the site that the person is happy being public. If in doubt, ask them first. Leaving a field blank just hides it.

Add a photo — and fix it when the head is cut off

Anywhere you see a photo box, click it and choose a picture from your phone or computer. Photos are shrunk automatically, so a big phone photo is fine.

If the top of someone's head is cut off: that's what the sliders under the photo are for. Photos get trimmed to fit a fixed shape, and the sliders choose which part you keep — left/right and up/down. Drag until the face is where you want it, then Apply Changes.

A photo taken portrait (tall) usually needs the up/down slider. One taken landscape (wide) usually needs left/right.

Change the welcome message on the homepage

Dashboard → Homepage. Edit the welcome heading and the message underneath, then Apply Changes.

The panel on the right shows how it'll look as you type. Nothing goes live until you click Apply Changes and confirm.

Change the church address, phone, or email

Dashboard → Contact Info. Change what you need, then Apply Changes.

This updates the Contact page and the footer at the bottom of every page at once.

Read prayer requests

Dashboard → Prayer Requests. Only Pastor Ladd and Full Admin accounts can open this — "Can Edit" and "View Only" accounts can't see it at all.

Each request shows whether the person agreed to have it read aloud in the prayer meeting. If it says no, it stays between you and the prayer team. Please take that seriously — people trust us with hard things.

If someone asks to have their request deleted, click Delete on it. We promise that on the privacy page, so it matters that we actually do it.

Set my own password

Dashboard → Settings → Your Own Password.

  1. Type the password you log in with now.
  2. Pick a new one — at least 8 characters.
  3. Type it again to confirm, then click Set My Password.

From then on it's yours alone. Nobody can see it, not even Dante — the site stores a scrambled version it can check against but can't read back.

Worth doing. Until you set your own, you're sharing one password with everyone, which means it can't be changed without telling the whole team.

Set up the authenticator app (no more waiting for emails)

Install Google Authenticator or Microsoft Authenticator on your phone — both free.

  1. Dashboard → Settings → Your Authenticator App → Set Up Authenticator App.
  2. Open the app on your phone, tap +, and scan the square code on screen.
  3. Next time you log in, pick "authenticator app" and type the 6-digit number it shows.

The number changes every 30 seconds. It works with no signal and no email, and it's quicker than waiting for a code to arrive.

If you get a new phone, set this up again before wiping the old one. If it's already gone, ask a Full Admin.

I forgot my password / I'm locked out

Ask a Full Admin. On Admin Users they click Reset their password next to your name. That puts you back on the shared church password so you can get in, then set a new one of your own in Settings.

If it says too many incorrect attempts, that's the site protecting your account. Wait about 15 minutes and try again — it clears by itself, and it only affects you, not anyone else at the church.

Add someone to the admin team (or remove them)

Dashboard → Admin Users. Full Admin only.

Click + Add Admin, put in their name and the email they'll log in with, and choose what they can do:

  • Full Admin — everything, including prayer requests and this list.
  • Can Edit — all the normal editing, but no prayer requests and no changing who's an admin.
  • View Only — can look at the Calendar and Gallery, can't change anything.

Use their personal email, not a shared church one — login codes should reach one person, not an inbox several people read.

Give people the least they need. It's not distrust; it just means a mistake or a stolen phone does less damage.

Anyone not on this list can't log in at all, even with the right password.

I changed something by mistake — can I undo it?

Yes. Dashboard → Change Log. Every change ever made is listed with who made it and when.

  1. Find the change.
  2. Click Preview before/after to see exactly what changed.
  3. Click Revert to before to put it back.

Text and lists come back exactly. Photos don't — if the change included a new photo you'll need to upload the old one again.

Put photos in the Gallery

Dashboard → Gallery. Add photos and Apply Changes.

The public Gallery page asks people to log in first, so these aren't visible to the whole internet — it's for sharing photos back with the congregation.

Before posting photos with people in them, especially children, make sure you have permission. Once something's online it's hard to take back.

Put out a newsletter

Dashboard → Newsletter. Start a new issue, add the pastor's message and as many articles as you like, each with a photo if you want one.

It stays a draft — invisible to everyone else — until you publish it. So you can work on it over several days without anyone seeing a half-finished page.

Make a page for a ministry or group

Dashboard → Ministries. Create one, give it a name, a description, and photos.

Each ministry gets its own page and appears on the Ministries list. Like the newsletter, it stays hidden until you publish it.

Change the photos behind the homepage welcome

Dashboard → Homepage Photos. Add a few and they'll slowly fade between each other behind the welcome text. Leave it empty for a plain background.

Wide (landscape) photos work best. Use the slider under each to choose which part shows.

Why hasn't my change appeared on the website?

Work through these in order:

  1. Did you click Apply Changes? Nothing goes live until you do. This is the usual answer.
  2. Did you confirm? Some screens ask twice on purpose.
  3. Refresh the page you're looking at — hold Shift and click reload.
  4. Check the Change Log. If your change isn't listed, it didn't save. If it is listed, it saved and you're looking at an old copy.
  5. Check the top of your screen for "Logged in as … (View Only)". View Only accounts can't save — a Full Admin can change that on Admin Users.
Something's broken / who do I ask?

Ask Dante McLaughlin. When you do, three things make it much quicker to fix:

  • A screenshot of what you're seeing.
  • What you clicked just before it happened.
  • Whether it happens every time or just once.

You can't break this by clicking around. Everything is recorded and reversible, and there's nothing on these screens that deletes anything without asking first. Explore.
